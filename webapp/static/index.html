Reinforcement learning for solar battery dispatch

What you are seeing

GréineQ replays a historical solar-battery day in 30-minute steps. At each step the agent sees solar generation, household demand, battery charge level, and grid price — then chooses hold, charge from solar, discharge to the home, grid-charge (buy), or export (sell). Grid-charge and export enable price arbitrage: buy low, use or sell later.
